Privacy policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026.

rclone-app is a personal OAuth client used only by its owner, through the open-source tool rclone, to upload files to the owner's own Google Drive.

  • What it accesses: only the Google Drive files it created itself (the drive.file scope). It cannot see any other files in the Drive.
  • What it collects: nothing. There is no server, analytics or tracking. The sign-in token stays in rclone's configuration file on the owner's own computer.
  • What it shares: nothing is sold or passed to anyone. Files are shared only when the owner chooses to create a share link for them.
  • Removing access: access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Questions: open an issue at github.com/AgIsh0203/rclone-app.
